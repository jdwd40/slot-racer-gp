# Laps of fuel left (#87)

The fuel meter shows an **approximate** laps-of-fuel figure right of the bar, beside the percent on the bar: `≈ 3.4 laps` on desktop and `≈3.4` on compact and phone layouts (the full "≈ 3.4 laps" is the element's `title` and accessible name). It is a reading of how far the tank goes at the car's **recent** burn, not a guarantee: lift and the figure grows, attack and it shrinks.

- **Where.** `src/fuelLaps.ts`, a pure client module used only by `src/hud.ts` and `test/fuelLaps.test.ts`. The race core, `src/net/` and `server/` never import it, so burn, refuelling, pit planning, last-chance and DQ timing, `LOW_FUEL`, the server bundle, `PROTOCOL_VERSION` (2) and `RULES_VERSION` are untouched. Copy: `hud.fuelLaps`, `hud.fuelLapsShort` in `assets/ui/strings.json`.
- **Estimate.** Burn in the race core is per distance times throttle (`abs(v)·dt·fuelRate(L)·(0.5 + 0.7·thr)`), so the HUD measures fuel burned per metre of slot (`Car.u`) and reports `laps = fuel / (burnPerDistance · L)`. Burn and distance are banked in quarter-lap chunks; the rate is the newest chunks covering about one lap (rolling, so a change of style shows within a lap). A stint needs half a lap of measured distance before its own rate is used.
- **Fallback.** Before that (grid, countdown, first half-lap) the rate is `fuelRate(L) × 1.1` (`FALLBACK_BURN`): a full tank reads ≈ 6.7 laps, between nominal 1.0 (7.4) and full throttle 1.2 (6.2).
- **Refill.** Fuel more than 0.001 above the lowest reading since the last resync is a refill: the stint (including the unfinished in-lap chunk) is dropped and the last observed rate is kept until the new stint has half a lap of its own. A stopped-on-track recovery bay adds no fuel, so it is not a refill.
- **Ignored.** Chunks whose implied multiplier is outside 0.4–2.0× nominal (stall recovery, tow, garbled data). Backward moves up to 2 m (the 1.6 m marshal re-slot, reversing) add no distance. A bigger jump, a race-clock gap over 0.35 s, or a fuel drop the distance cannot explain resyncs: nothing across it is counted.
- **Online.** The HUD runs on the client mirror, which has `fuel` (3 decimals, a non-empty tank never 0, #79), `u`, `lapsDone`, `crossed` and the race clock from the existing snapshots: no new fields. Burn is measured as the drop over a whole chunk, so the 0.001 quantum never accumulates; a 0.001 flicker is not a refill; a reconnect or missed snapshots resync.
- **Shown.** One decimal, changing at most twice a second of race clock. Hidden in practice, once the car has finished or is out (DQ, DNF), and after the flag. An empty tank in a live race reads `≈ 0.0`.
- **Colour** (text only, no chip, no blink: the bar already blinks below 20%). Orange when the figure is below the laps still to drive to the flag including the rest of this lap (`(LAPS − lapsDone) − ahead(FINISH_S, s)/L`, the distance `fuelToFinish` plans with, without the AI's margin; on the grid, before the first crossing, the run-up to the line is added to the full race). Red under 1.0 lap. Otherwise the muted HUD grey.
